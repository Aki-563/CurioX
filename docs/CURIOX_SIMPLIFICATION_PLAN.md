## Proposed structure

- Header: CurioX wordmark linking Home; Products, About, Contact. Add Products only when its real route is ready. No additional header CTA.
- Home: approved Curious Minds. Creating Tomorrow. headline; one factual sentence; View products CTA; a compact Smriti preview using approved details; compact contact row; footer.
- Products `/products/`: Products heading, optional one-line introduction, project-card grid. Start with one Smriti card; do not fabricate extra projects or empty slots to fill columns. One card should fit comfortably without forcing a three-column layout.
- About: concise company introduction plus purpose/approach. Suggested factual mission sentence: We explore emerging technology and build useful software, with a focus on approachability and affordability.
- Contact: heading, short invitation, approved email link.
- 404: retain functional recovery route; short message and Home link suffice.

Keep the separate About and Contact routes for now so the product catalog stays focused. Removing those routes would be a separate architecture/navigation decision, not essential to the cleanup.
