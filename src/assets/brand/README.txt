CURIOX / final logo collection / 22 November 2026

CONTENTS
32 logo variants in total: 19 with backgrounds and 13 transparent.

BACKGROUND VERSIONS
19 SVG logo files and 19 matching one-page vector PDFs:
- Symbol only, horizontal, and vertical arrangements in five treatments:
  black on white; white on black; white on #9351F3; purple on white;
  purple on black. These are the 15 single-color versions.
- Horizontal and vertical brand-color lockups: purple symbol plus black
  wordmark on white, or purple symbol plus white wordmark on black.
  These are four additional two-color versions.

curiox-complete-logos.pdf: 36 pages - four overview pages plus 32 vector artboards.
curiox-logo-overview.pdf: two-page visual index of all 19 versions.

All requested backgrounds are included as full-artboard filled rectangles.
All logo lettering is outlined. No installed fonts are needed to use the
SVG or PDF artwork. PDFs retain scalable paths; they contain no raster logos.

APPROVED DESIGN
Wordmark: CURIOX, Space Grotesk Bold, uniform type size, tracking -0.025 em.
Symbol: original D2 curves with the aligned dot. The mirrored experiment is
not used. The approved source mark checksum is retained in manifest.json.
Do not independently move or resize the dot, stretch artwork, or substitute
a different wordmark case. Resize the complete SVG/PDF uniformly.

COLORS
Black #000000 / RGB 0,0,0
White #FFFFFF / RGB 255,255,255
Accent #9351F3 / RGB 147,81,243
Files use digital sRGB values. Use a printer-approved color conversion and
proof when preparing process-color print production.

Space Grotesk is distributed under the included SIL Open Font License.
This collection records design approval; trademark clearance is unverified.

TRANSPARENT ADDITION
The transparent/ folder adds 13 variants in SVG, vector PDF, and PNG.
All 32 logo versions are included in the updated complete PDF.
Transparent SVG and PNG files have no background; transparent PDFs have no
painted background. Preview checkerboards are not included in the assets.
See transparent/README.txt for the full file guide.
